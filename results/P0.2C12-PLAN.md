# P0.2C12 — exact IWM mode and full-width RDDATA pulses

P0.2C11 proves the entire control path for the first time:

- FujiNet receives the `$7FA55A` arm.
- The IIgs produces the routed `1011` READY phase.
- FujiNet acquires the SPI bus and completes the private transmit with
  `err=0`.
- The IIgs nevertheless sees no marker.

The remaining scope is the physical waveform and IWM Read Data latch.

P0.2C12 changes two receive-path variables together:

1. The host no longer accepts C-only mode `$0C`. With internal Slot 6 now
   correctly routed, it programs and requires the documented complete `$0F`
   mode, including asynchronous timing and full-byte latch bits. A failure
   reports both the saved and active mode.
2. The private FujiNet encoder remains at a 2 MHz SPI clock and retains 2-us
   falling-edge spacing, but widens asserted RDDATA pulses from one SPI sample
   (0.5 us) to two samples (1 us). Normal 4-us SmartPort transmission remains
   untouched.

Expected success trace:

```text
FASTIWM C12 READY TRIGGER ... phase=0b
FASTIWM C12 TX SHAPE ... cell=2us pulse=1us
FASTIWM TX DONE ... err=0
```

and on the IIgs:

```text
FAST PASS: exact 512-byte 2us payload verified.
```
