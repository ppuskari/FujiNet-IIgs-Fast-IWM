# P0.2D7 guarded SPI and GS/OS exit candidate

P0.2D6 completed 14,279 exact 16 KiB batches before a rare failure at the
start of batch 14,280. The matching FujiNet ELF identifies an interrupt
watchdog race: a duplicate phase-1011 GPIO interrupt entered normal SmartPort
SPI receive while the private 2-us transmitter owned the same SPI bus.

P0.2D7 adds two paired corrections:

- FujiNet treats every additional 1011 interrupt during a private burst as
  part of that burst and never lets it enter normal SmartPort receive.
- Active serial diagnostics are removed from the phase ISR and summarized
  later from normal service context.
- Every 256 completed batches, FujiNet reports free, minimum-free, and largest
  8-bit heap blocks plus duplicate-READY and deferred-ISR counters.
- The IIgs restores `DISKREG`, `SLTROMSEL`, IWM mode, and CPU speed after every
  successful batch without issuing the expensive 0101 SmartPort reset.

The last item prevents a later transport failure from returning GS/OS with
the temporary internal-Slot-6/3.5-inch mapping still selected. The observed
`BRK $64` at `$00/C603` was GS/OS entering that incorrectly mapped Slot-6 ROM.

## Soak checkpoints

1. Confirm normal startup, provider buffering, DOC playback, and channel
   changes.
2. Confirm `FASTIWM D7 HEAP` remains bounded across multiple reports.
3. Cross 14,280 completed batches (about 2 hours 57 minutes at 21,973 Hz).
4. Stop normally and confirm clean return to GS/OS.
5. Disconnect or reset FujiNet during playback, then confirm the client
   reports failure and still returns cleanly to GS/OS.
