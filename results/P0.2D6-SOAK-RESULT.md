FujiNet firmware on 2026-08-22.

- Provider bytes: 84,969,472
- Equivalent audio time at 21,973 bytes/second: approximately 64 minutes 27 seconds
- Lifetime provider rate: 175.9 kbit/second

than 5.6 times. The station-change test also exercised live provider source
handoff while the IIgs DOC ring, FujiNet batch transport, and TCP connection
remained active.
